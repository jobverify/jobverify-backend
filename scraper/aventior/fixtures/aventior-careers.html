Careers At Aventior

Opportunities to Engineer New Possibilities.

At Aventior, we develop platforms and solutions for Health Practitioners using Computer Vision-based detection and AI-backed analysis and propagation. With AI, we see a better rate of sensitivity and accuracy in detecting chronic irregularities. With the help of AI and deep learning in clinical studies, our platforms are capable of better interpretation and ruling out false alarms and, at the same time, can raise a flag in case of an irregularity that would have normally been missed in case of manual interpretation.

Our Focus On Diversity & Equal Opportunity

Diversity is at the heart of our dedicated team of experts. We bring together individuals with varying subject matter knowledge and a rich mix of nationalities and ethnicities. Another key principle in our team development is equal opportunity. Since our inception, we have prioritized maintaining a healthy balance of people of all genders in our teams.

Join us in this journey and help us take the steps in engineering new possibilities as Team Aventior enriches your growth and career roadmap.
