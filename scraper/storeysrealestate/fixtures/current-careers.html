Why we exist

We were built by people the industry overlooked.

Dubai real estate has a reputation, and not all of it is kind. Agents who disappear after the sale. Numbers that only work in the brochure. Teams where you are one of forty and nobody knows your name by Friday.

Storeys was founded by people who had worked inside exactly that and decided to run a brokerage the other way round. Salaried consultants instead of commission-only hunters, so that nobody has to push a client into the wrong unit to make rent. Direct access to developers, so that what we show is real. And a leadership team that still sits on the same floor as everybody else.

That is the reason this page exists. If you want to do this work properly, there is a place here for you, and there is a path.

What it is like here

A culture that changes what people are capable of.

01

Nobody arrives finished

Product training on every developer we work with, role plays before you sit in front of a client, and a senior colleague who takes your call when a deal wobbles. We would rather teach the market to a good person than hire a difficult one who already knows it.

02

A real start for first-time agents

If this is your first step, you are not handed a phone and a list. You get onboarding, a defined patch, live inventory from day one, and someone whose job it is to answer your questions.

03

Room for people who already know

Experienced agents come to us for what decides a year at their level: direct developer allocations, launches before they reach the open market, a brand clients already trust, and a back office that lets them spend the day selling.
