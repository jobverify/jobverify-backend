Frequently asked questions

Who can apply to #NextGen programmes?

Our internships are typically for students in their penultimate year of study, while graduate roles are for those who have recently completed their degree. Specific requirements may vary by role, so please check individual job descriptions for details.

Do I need a specific degree or background?

This depends on the role. Some positions may require a relevant degree, while others are open to a broader range of backgrounds. We’re interested in your skills, mindset, and potential.

When do applications open?

Application timelines vary by programme, team, and location. We recommend checking our careers site regularly and applying early where possible.

Can I apply to more than one role?

Yes. You can apply to multiple roles if they align with your skills and interests. We recommend focusing on the areas where you’re most motivated to make an impact.

Do you offer internships and early talent programme roles in all locations?

Opportunities vary by team and location. Not all programmes are available everywhere, so please check what’s open in your preferred location.

Are internships paid?

Yes. All of our internships are paid and designed to provide meaningful, hands-on experience.

Is there an opportunity to join full-time after an internship?

High-performing interns may be considered for full-time opportunities, depending on business needs and available roles.

Do you offer visa sponsorship?

Visa support depends on the role and location. Please refer to individual job descriptions for specific details.

What support will I receive during the programme?

You’ll have access to onboarding, training, and support from your manager and team, along with opportunities to learn from experienced colleagues and connect with the wider #NextGen community.
