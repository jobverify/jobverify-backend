Employee benefits

Benefits of working with us

Along with your salary, our employee benefits support you in your work and in your life, and they help you grow in your career with us.

Health insurance

We give you medical insurance so that you can get the care you need when you are not well.

Learning programs

We run regular training sessions and programs that help you learn new skills and tools.

Team celebrations

We celebrate festivals, special days and team successes together during the year.

Shift allowances

We pay extra to team members who work in night shifts or rotating shifts for our global clients.

Performance based rewards

Course certification reimbursement

02

Resume review

Our hiring team checks your resume against the needs of the job and gets back to you within a few working days.

03

Technical and HR interviews

You will have one or two rounds of interviews where we talk about your experience, how you solve problems and how well you match the role.

04

Meet the team

You will meet the team members and leaders that you will work with every day.
