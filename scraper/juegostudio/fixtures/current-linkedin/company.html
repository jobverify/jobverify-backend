About us

Juego Studios is a full-cycle game development and co-development partner for studios and publishers worldwide. Founded in 2013, we operate in the UK, KSA, and the US — as Juego Studios Inc — with production teams based in India. With 250+ specialists across art, engineering, design, and LiveOps, we've shipped 200+ projects over 10+ years. We're ISO 9001:2015 certified, with long-term partnerships including Sony, Disney, Tencent, Warner Bros., Zynga, Amazon Studios, Gameloft, Scopely, Games Workshop, and Big Fish Games. We're listed in Forbes India's Marquee Entrepreneur Special and featured in APAC CIO Outlook for excellence in immersive technology services. Services Game Development & Production — Unity & Unreal, Mobile/PC/Console, JAI (proprietary AI prototyping framework) Game Art — AAA art, 3D modeling & sculpting, character/environment design, 2D & 3D animation, UI/UX Game Porting — cross-platform porting (mobile, PC, Switch, console) with certification & compliance Game LiveOps — content updates, seasonal events, KPI-bound optimization (D1/D7/D30 retention, ARPDAU, crash-free rate) Engagement Models Full-Cycle Game Development — concept through LiveOps, including all asset creation Co-Development — integrates with your existing team on a defined module or pipeline Team Augmentation — dedicated resources under your direct supervision
