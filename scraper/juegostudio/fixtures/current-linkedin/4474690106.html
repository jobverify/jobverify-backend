      Talent Acquisition Specialist | End-to-End IT, Non-IT, Creative & Niche Hiring| High-Volume Recruitment | Recruitment Process Automation |…

      We are looking for a concept artist who can Create strong visual concepts for game

      characters, environments, buildings, vehicles, props, and UI-related assets. Move from

      research and thumbnails to polished, production-ready artwork. Practical knowledge of AI

      art tools and structured prompting is a key requirement. Interpret briefs, control style and

      composition through prompts, and refine outputs with sound art fundamentals and

      Photoshop skills


      Responsibilities

      • Create thumbnails, moodboards, layouts, sketches, and polished concepts for

      game characters, environments, buildings, vehicles, and props.

      • Research relevant art styles and comparable games, then present visual references

      and concepts to the project team.

      • Collaborate with the team to interpret briefs and develop artwork in the format each

      project requires, including 2D concepts art or 3D related concept.

      • Use AI tools and structured prompts to explore ideas efficiently, then refine the

      results through artistic editing and quality checks.

      • Incorporate client and team feedback while maintaining consistent perspective,

      proportions, lighting, colour, and style across assets.

      • Manage assigned timelines, document feedback, and raise unclear requirements or

      delays early.

      • Stay current with game art techniques, design trends, and production tools.


      Requirements

      • 2–3 years of concept art or game art experience, supported by a strong portfolio.

      • Strong drawing, painting, composition, colour, and design fundamentals.

      • Hands-on experience with AI image tools and the ability to write clear,

      controlled prompts for different game asset types.

      • Ability to identify and correct AI-generated errors, including anatomy, perspective,

      object consistency, and unwanted details.

      • Strong Photoshop skills, including layers, masks, paint-over work, and organized

      production files.

      • Ability to take an asset from a rough idea through to a clean, usable final design.

      • Good communication, attention to detail, and the ability to work with feedback and

      deadlines.

      • A degree or certification in Fine Arts or a related field is welcome, but portfolio

      quality and practical skills are more important
