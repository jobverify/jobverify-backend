The values we live for

⭐

Challenge the status quo

Don’t follow the default path. Question what’s given, think for yourself, and try something different. Real progress comes from people who challenge how things are done.

⚡️

Act fast

Move with urgency and a bias for action. You don’t need perfect information to start. Speed matters—and so does the willingness to adapt as you go.

🔍

Seek the truth

Go deeper than surface answers. Ask better questions, stay curious, and form your own point of view. Clarity comes from those who care enough to dig.

🎯

Own the outcome

Take responsibility from start to finish. Be clear on what you’re solving and why it matters. Wins and losses—own them both.

🤝

Earn trust

Trust isn't given—it's earned. Say what you'll do, do what you say. Users rely on us, teammates rely on each other. Build it in small moments, and never take it for granted.

❤️

User love always wins

Build for real people, not just metrics. If it genuinely helps and delights users, you’re on the right track. When users win, we win.
