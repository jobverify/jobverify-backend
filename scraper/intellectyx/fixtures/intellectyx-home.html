Why Enterprise AI Stalls

The Pilot Is 10% of the Work.

The distance between a working demo and a production system is not model quality. It is eight missing layers, and a weakness in any one of six pillars.

The Other 90%

Identity & entitlements

Agents act as a user, inside that user’s permissions

Enterprise integration

Systems of record, not exports and sandboxes

Grounded knowledge

Owned sources, lineage, citations

Evaluation harness

Golden sets, regression before release

Observability & tracing

Every step and decision, retained

Cost & capacity control

Budgets and ceilings per workflow

Policy & guardrails

Policy-as-code, risk tiering, audit

Human-in-the-loop

Checkpoints, escalation, reversal

All eight ship with the IX AI Foundry.

Every failure maps to one of six pillars

01

Business

Value case, P&L ownership, funding, benefit tracking

02

People

Sponsorship, operating model, roles, adoption, change

03

Data

AI-ready data, lineage, quality, access, retrieval

04

Technology

Reference architecture, integration, agent platform

05

Operations

LLMOps, AgentOps, evaluation, observability, run model

06

Governance

Security, risk, compliance, responsible AI, audit

Maturity is set by your weakest pillar, not your strongest. AI is a chain, and it breaks where it is thinnest.

Executive Takeaway

Budget for the other 90%, and score all six pillars before you fund the next use case. Fund the weakest, not the loudest.
