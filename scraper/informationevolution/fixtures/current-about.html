Corporate Social Responsibility

Giving back to our communities, and supporting the fields advancing our digital future.

Austin, Texas

AAAI - When the Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI) held its annual meeting in Austin, Information Evolution was proud to support their efforts to recognize and accelerate innovation in the field.
aaai.org

KNOWBILITY - Knowbility is a nonprofit organization based in Austin, Texas, dedicated to accessible information technology. Their mission is to create an inclusive digital world for people with disabilities, and Information Evolution has supported them since 2022.
knowbility.org/about

UNIVERSITY of TEXAS at AUSTIN - SCHOOL of INFORMATION UT - Austin's iSchool is one of the world's premier graduate education and research institutions focused on how society uses technology to disseminate information. Our CEO was a member of the iSchool's Advisory Board.
ischool.utexas.edu

Coimbatore, India

ROTARY INTERNATIONAL - The Rotary Club of Coimbatore purchased ventilation equipment, supplies, and services for government-run hospitals in Coimbatore in 2020, with support from Information Evolution. IE has continuously supported the Coimbatore chapter's annual fundraising event every year since 2019. Our CTO is an officer and active member of the local chapter.
rotary.org
facebook.com/rccspectrum

ALERT - EMERGENCY RESPONSE TRAINING & PARTNERSHIP - Alert is a Chennai-based non-profit that trains people in emergency response and first-aid, working to improve the emergency response ecosystem in India. Information Evolution has supported the cause since 2020, and our President is Alert-certified and serves as their Global Voice Ambassador.
In 2022, Information Evolution sponsored Alert NGO's Kovai event, covering tickets for all employees who participated as runners and volunteers in Alertathon — the largest endurance event dedicated to saving lives, now in its 8th edition.
alert.ngo

Coonoor, India

NILGIRIS DISTRICT – COVID RELIEF EFFORT - In 2020, Information Evolution partnered with the Nilgiris District Administration and local NGOs to support COVID relief in the district. This included funding the purchase of medical equipment for treating COVID patients at local health centers, providing relief material for less privileged communities in the area, and creating employment opportunities for seasonally displaced workers in Coonoor.

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT IN THE NILGIRIS - OOTY ULTRA PARTNERSHIP - Information Evolution has partnered with Ooty Ultra since 2018, supporting community development in the Nilgiris as a corporate sponsor. Alongside other contributors, we've backed two play park initiatives: the Kenthorai Play Park (October 2024) and the Dhoddanni Panchayat Union Public School Play Park (February 2026) — both creating inclusive spaces for children in underserved communities.

The Dhoddanni project holds particular significance: located in an interior area of the Nilgiris, the Panchayat Union Public School is the only accessible school for children in the surrounding communities, and the new play park gives them a safe space that supports their development and encourages school participation.

While we're not directly involved in organizing Ooty Ultra events, we're proud to see our employees actively taking part in the running community.

Nuvali, Philippines

ROTARY PARTNERSHIP - Information Evolution is a proud Gold Sponsor of the "18 Legs Golf Tournament" (20 August 2026), a fundraising initiative by Rotary. Proceeds from the event support vital welfare projects across Rotary International's seven avenues of service: promoting peace, fighting disease, providing access to clean water, protecting the health of mothers and children, advancing education, strengthening local economies, and safeguarding the environment.
