Compensations and Benefits

At Lexmark, people come first. We strive to look after you and your family, hence, we got you covered. We offer a competitive salary, attractive insurance plans, and a range of benefits to make working with us a fulfilling experience.

Room for Growth

We inspire you to grow and develop as your success is important to us. We encourage you to choose a career path that is as unique as you are. Be a manager or a technical expert — Lexmark provides the opportunity to shine your aspiration.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Lexmark is committed to contribute as a responsible corporate citizen. We provide a platform for our people to give back to the community in which we live and work. As we strive to maintain best practices for sustainability, we conduct programs and activities to cater to the humanitarian responses and social concerns to make a difference in the society.

Work-Life Balance

Work-life balance is especially important at Lexmark. We promote employees’ holistic well-being. We offer flexible working hours and a generous leave program to help balance your personal needs with business requirements. We understand that when you are happy and healthy, you perform better. 

Founded in 1991, Lexmark is recognised as a global leader in imaging and output technology solutions and managed print services by many of the technology industry’s leading market analyst firms. Lexmark sells its products and services in more than 170 countries and has its headquarters in Lexington, Kentucky.

Lexmark India, located in Kolkata, is one of the research and development centers of Lexmark International Inc. Our diverse workforce collaborates their skills and ideas to build, deliver, and support world-class products and solutions for our customers.

Know more ​

In Lexmark India, we do more than a job. We celebrate adventure in innovation. Every year we organize engaging and motivating sessions like Hackathon, Focus 2 Future (F2F), Technical Group Mentorship (TGM), Technical Education and Cultivation Hub (TEACH) with substantial enthusiastic participation from India R&D teams to collaborate knowledge, pursue a diverse range of perspectives, and amalgamate ideas. The best innovative projects get awarded at the end of these events.

Any guess what could be more exciting? We encourage our practitioners to contribute to the prestigious Lexmark Patent Portfolio! We uphold the culture of giving out the Inventor award to all the achievers. So, if you are the tech expert who is all set to use your skills, knowledge, and experience to lead others as well as to rediscover your creative genius, join us for your dream job and make a difference.
 

Lexmark's success is driven by the company's overarching commitment to its customers. Cost-effective and continuous achievement of customer satisfaction is the foundation of our business. With a corporate vision of "Customers For Life," we earn our customers' loyalty by listening to them, anticipating their needs, and working to create value for them. This exceptional customer engagement results in customer loyalty rates that exceed 95 percent across our worldwide customer base.

Our products are sold in more than 170 countries in North and South America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia, the Pacific Rim, and the Caribbean.

At Lexmark, we value reliability, flexibility, responsiveness, innovative products, and exemplary services. We believe growth, longevity, and financial success will naturally follow if we live our core concepts every day. We strive to maintain best practices for sustainability — balancing economic, environmental, and social concerns.

We are committed to be a socially responsible company with a strong respect for human rights, safe work conditions, and environmentally sound business practices, for our own organisation and those with whom we partner. We honor cultural diversity and the unique perspective of each individual.
